Five Things Worth Checking

A supplement should be evaluated based on what is actually disclosed about the product—not exaggerated promises.

01 — Ingredients

Can you easily find the ingredients included in the formula?

02 — Transparency

Does the manufacturer clearly explain what the product is?

03 — Routine

Is the intended daily use clearly explained?

04 — Manufacturer

Is there clear information about the company behind the product?

05 — Purchase Terms

Are pricing, guarantee and purchase conditions clearly presented?

06 — Realistic Expectations

Be cautious of products making extreme or unrealistic promises.
