Five things worth checking

A better supplement decision starts with information you can actually verify.

1

Ingredient transparency

Look for a clearly presented ingredient list rather than vague proprietary language that makes comparison difficult.

2

Serving clarity

The recommended serving should be straightforward so you understand how the product is intended to be used.

3

Product information

Check whether the manufacturer, product details and relevant consumer information are easy to locate.

4

Purchase terms

Before buying, review the current price, shipping details and refund or guarantee terms presented by the seller.

5

Realistic expectations

Be cautious with pages that promise extraordinary or guaranteed results. Marketing language should not replace evidence.

6

Do your own research

Use product information as one input. If you have health questions or take medication, speak with an appropriately qualified professional.
