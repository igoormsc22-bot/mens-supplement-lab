A simpler way to compare your options

You don't need to know everything about supplements. Start with a few objective details and use them to make a more informed comparison.

01

Formula transparency

Look for clear information about the product formula rather than relying only on marketing language.

02

Ingredient information

Review the information provided about the ingredients and how they are presented.

03

Serving details

Check the stated serving size and directions so you know exactly what the product page says.

04

Product format

Consider whether the format and routine are practical for your preferences.

05

Purchase details

Before ordering, review the current product, pricing and purchase information on the seller's page.

06

Realistic expectations

Be cautious with extraordinary or guaranteed claims. Marketing language should not replace evidence.
